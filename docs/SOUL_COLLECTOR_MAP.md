# Soul Collector: BG3 creature to FFT monster mapping (APPROVED by the user 2026-10-13; Act 1 souls built in 1.0.0.42)

Written 2026-10-13 by the scheduled run while the user was away. **Nothing here is built.** PHASE2.md item 14: "Propose
the full mapping table to the user before building the forms."

Each devoured BG3 creature type gives **two forms**: the BG3 creature itself (with new skills scaled to its power) and
the FFT monster below. Sources: the FFT monsters and their TIC names and descriptions come from the vanilla TIC table
`Job-en` on the user's PC. The BG3 creature types come from the extracted vanilla `Character.txt` stats.

**Tiers.** Every FFT family has three members, early to late in TIC's own order (for example Chocobo → Black Chocobo
→ Red Chocobo). Act 1 creatures get the first member, Act 2 the second, Act 3 the third, unless the theme says
otherwise (the user's rule: theme **and** tier).

## The user's answers (2026-10-13)

1. **Soul Brand replaces the killing blow (new rule):** the Soul Collector puts a debuff on an enemy: **1 turn, can't
   be resisted, no cooldown**. If the branded enemy dies while branded (by anyone's hand), the party devours its soul.
2. **Unmatched FFT monsters** (Elder Treant, Blue Dragon, Hydra, Greater Hydra, Tiamat) sound like powerful forms:
   **attach them to Act 3 creatures**, but **build the Act 1 forms first**.
3. **The nearest fits are fine** (spiders → Coeurl, imps → Ahriman, giant badgers → Swine, ogres → Sekhret).
4. **Lucavi forms for the big villains: yes.**
5. The construct Soul form keeps the name **Automaton**; the special job is Steel Giant.

## The choices that were asked

1. **FFT monsters with no BG3 match yet:** Elder Treant, Blue Dragon and the whole hydra family (Hydra, Greater Hydra,
   Tiamat): BG3 has no hydras. Leave them out, or give them to elite Act 3 creatures?
2. **Nearest-fit choices:** spiders → Coeurl, imps → Ahriman, giant badgers → Swine, ogres → Sekhret. Fine?
3. **Not devourable:** ordinary humanoid enemies (duergar, drow, githyanki, cultists, bandits) unless they are bosses
   (PHASE2.md item 12). Earth and air elementals, devas and meazels are left out for now.
4. **Name clash:** "Automaton" is both a Soul form here and TIC's name for Worker 8's special job. Proposal: the special
   job keeps War of the Lions' name **Steel Giant**, and the Soul form keeps **Automaton**.
5. **Lucavi forms only for the big villains** (Minthara, Ketheric, Gortash, Orin, Sarevok, Raphael, the Elder Brain):
   OK?

## Notes for building it later (PHASE2.md step 5)

- Identify the creature type from the dead creature's stats/template (SE `Ext.Entity` on the `KilledBy` target); the
  BG3 types above are vanilla `Character.txt` entry families (`Owlbear*`, `Gnoll*`, `Spider_Phase*`, ...).
- Forms work like Wild Shape (the decided rules in PHASE2.md items 15-17: level-scaled, 2 charges per short rest,
  essences 1 / 3 / 5); research BG3's Wild Shape statuses first.
- The "Soul Known" marker goes on enemies whose type the party already devoured.
